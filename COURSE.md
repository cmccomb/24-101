# 24-101 course plan

14 instructional weeks. Projects run sequentially for 3, 4 and 7 weeks. Every Monday introduces a different mechanical system through a demonstration, calculation breakdown and design considerations. Wednesdays teach product design and development methods that directly support the active project. The textbook supplies a content spine rather than a chapter-by-chapter syllabus. Specific project topics remain open.

[Interactive systems](https://cmccomb.com/24-101/) · [Course plan and coverage](https://cmccomb.com/24-101/course/)

## Project sequence

- **Project 1, weeks 1–3: Bounded design sprint.** A tested prototype, a measurable requirement and a short design rationale.
- **Project 2, weeks 4–7: Structured design project.** A justified concept, a prototype and evidence from a planned test.
- **Project 3, weeks 8–14: Integrated design project.** An integrated solution with stakeholder evidence, engineering analysis and documented iteration.

## Monday demonstrations, calculations and design

### 1. Laser cutter

- **Demonstrate:** Cut a kerf-fit coupon; measure slot fit against the drawing.
- **Calculate and design:** Compare line energy and removal demand, then examine kerf, stock thickness, joints and material selection.
- **Project connection:** Treat fit as a measurable user requirement.
- **MechE coverage:** Manufacturing and materials; Heat transfer; Numerical or experimental modeling; Human-centered product design.

### 2. FDM 3D printer

- **Demonstrate:** Compare the frame and spacer toolpaths on TechSpark’s Ender-3 rack profile.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate material throughput and heater demand; consider layer orientation, supports, tolerance and temperature feedback.
- **Project connection:** Use a fabrication constraint to revise the prototype.
- **MechE coverage:** Manufacturing and materials; Heat transfer; Sensing and actuation; Controls; Numerical or experimental modeling.

### 3. Bicycle brake and wheel

- **Demonstrate:** Spin a wheel and apply a disc brake; compare hand effort and stopping distance.
- **Calculate and design:** Draw the lever, rotor and tire free-body diagrams; calculate torque, grip limit, stopping distance and heat.
- **Project connection:** Translate hand comfort and stopping performance into requirements.
- **MechE coverage:** Statics and solids; Mechanisms; Dynamics and vibration; Human-centered product design.

### 4. Elevator

- **Demonstrate:** Raise a model car and counterweight with different payloads.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate rope tensions, axial stress and signed shaft power; consider braking, redundancy and accessible ride quality.
- **Project connection:** Separate stakeholder needs from technical specifications.
- **MechE coverage:** Statics and solids; Mechanisms; Sensing and actuation; Controls; Human-centered product design.

### 5. Robot arm and gripper

- **Demonstrate:** Command a joint step, vary gains, then lift a known mass with friction jaws.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate gravity torque, jaw force and feedback response; consider reach, compliance, sensing and pinch points.
- **Project connection:** Build a needs-to-specifications trace for the active concept.
- **MechE coverage:** Statics and solids; Mechanisms; Sensing and actuation; Controls; Numerical or experimental modeling; Human-centered product design.

### 6. Hydraulic press

- **Demonstrate:** Push two connected syringe pistons of different diameters; compare force and travel.
- **Calculate and design:** Apply Pascal’s principle, pressure-area force, displaced volume and work conservation; consider frame stress, seals and relief valves.
- **Project connection:** Compare concepts using the same load case and constraints.
- **MechE coverage:** Manufacturing and materials; Statics and solids; Mechanisms; Fluids; Sensing and actuation.

### 7. Centrifugal pump and piping loop

- **Demonstrate:** Measure flow and pressure in a transparent recirculating loop.
- **Calculate and design:** Intersect pump and system curves; compare static lift, pipe loss and input power with measurements.
- **Project connection:** Test the chosen concept against its measurable requirement.
- **MechE coverage:** Fluids; Sensing and actuation; Numerical or experimental modeling.

### 8. Refrigerator / heat pump

- **Demonstrate:** Measure cabinet and room temperatures and observe compressor cycling.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate heat leak, COP, cooling capacity and duty; reverse the heat-pump viewpoint and consider insulation and service access.
- **Project connection:** Define stakeholders and system boundaries for the integrated project.
- **MechE coverage:** Thermodynamics; Heat transfer; Controls; Numerical or experimental modeling; Human-centered product design.

### 9. Espresso machine

- **Demonstrate:** Time a water-only boiler warm-up and compare a pressure reading with brew-face force.
- **Calculate and design:** Break down water and boiler heat capacity, heat-up time and hydraulic power; consider cleaning, ergonomics and thermal control.
- **Project connection:** Use an order-of-magnitude estimate to screen concepts.
- **MechE coverage:** Thermodynamics; Heat transfer; Fluids; Sensing and actuation; Human-centered product design.

### 10. Washing machine suspension

- **Demonstrate:** Sweep the speed of an eccentric-mass rig through resonance.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate forced vibration, natural frequency and transmitted force; consider damping, isolation, balance and noise.
- **Project connection:** Allocate interfaces and performance budgets across subsystems.
- **MechE coverage:** Dynamics and vibration; Statics and solids; Numerical or experimental modeling; Human-centered product design.

### 11. Quadcopter

- **Demonstrate:** Use the simulation or a restrained rig to compare roll feedback gains.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate hover thrust and ideal induced power, then roll response; consider IMU feedback, mass placement and guards.
- **Project connection:** Plan repeatable tests and record a model-versus-test discrepancy.
- **MechE coverage:** Dynamics and vibration; Fluids; Sensing and actuation; Controls; Numerical or experimental modeling.

### 12. CMU Buggy

- **Demonstrate:** Compare coast-down predictions with a small cart or recorded freeroll data.
- **Calculate and design:** Account for gravitational energy, rolling loss and aerodynamic drag; consider braking, visibility, driver fit and structure.
- **Project connection:** Evaluate materials, fabrication and sustainability before final build.
- **MechE coverage:** Dynamics and vibration; Statics and solids; Fluids; Numerical or experimental modeling; Human-centered product design.

### 13. Autoinjector

- **Demonstrate:** Use a spring-driven bench syringe with a generic liquid and a visible plunger.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate spring force, pressure, viscous resistance and displacement; consider trigger force, indication and manufacturing tolerances.
- **Project connection:** Refine the design using cost, service and user evidence.
- **MechE coverage:** Mechanisms; Fluids; Statics and solids; Human-centered product design; Numerical or experimental modeling.

### 14. Data-center cooling system

- **Demonstrate:** Measure air temperatures across a heated box at several fan settings.
- **Calculate and design:** Calculate air heat balance and fan-power scaling; consider hot spots, recirculation, sensing and facility energy.
- **Project connection:** Explain an integrated tradeoff with measured evidence.
- **MechE coverage:** Thermodynamics; Heat transfer; Fluids; Sensing and actuation; Controls; Numerical or experimental modeling.
